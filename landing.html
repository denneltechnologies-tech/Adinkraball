Empowering Tomorrow's Leaders

Your Seat Funds a Scholarship

Proceeds from the Ball go directly to the GhPN Scholarship Program — merit-based awards for high school and undergraduate students recognized for academic, athletic, and artistic excellence.

Since inception we have awarded over $20,000 to fifteen students. At the 5th Edition, we are targeting $50,000 to multiply that impact across Ghana and the diaspora.
