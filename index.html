Organizational Profile

About the Ghanaian Professionals Network

A non-profit association dedicated to connecting, supporting, and empowering Ghanaian professionals to advance careers, businesses, and education interests at home and globally.

Executive Perspective

"Our collective progress depends on the strength of our connections."

Across Accra, Kumasi, London, Atlanta, and Dallas, Ghanaians are performing at the highest levels of global enterprise, medicine, and governance — yet too often our efforts remain fragmented. GhPN exists to unite this talent: when a partner mentors an aspiring lawyer, or an executive engages a vetted local enterprise from our directory, we create real economic momentum for our people.

Dr. Naa Borkor Quartey, FWACS Consultant Surgeon & Board President, GhPN Ghana

Our Mission

To mobilize Ghanaian professionals to support professional, academic, and personal growth through structured mentorship, educational scholarship, and collaborative economic networks.

Our Vision

A vibrant, collaborative global network of Ghanaian leaders shaping industry, academia, and civic institutions with integrity, mutual support, and national purpose.

Core Initiatives

Programs & Grants

Structured initiatives that elevate Ghanaian scholars and young graduates.

Endowed Grants

GhPN Academic Scholarship Fund

Full annual tuition grants, laptop equipment, and professional mentorship from matriculation to graduation for gifted Ghanaian students.

Institutions: UG Legon, KNUST, UCC, Ashesi & UHAS.
Eligibility: Undergraduate Level 200–400, 3.2 GPA / CWA 70%+, financial need & civic record.

Professional Development

Executive Mentorship Cohorts

Six-month cohorts pairing emerging professionals with senior executives — bi-weekly check-ins, career mapping, salary negotiation coaching, and research publishing guidance.

Focus areas: Software Engineering, Investment Banking, Public Health & Corporate Law.
Impact: 450+ mentor–mentee pairs completed, 4,000+ professional hours contributed annually.
